One line of plain text — a one-star review of Monday, written as though
Monday were a restaurant:

> One star. The server sat me at a wobbly table, served cold emails, and charged me my entire weekend.

Every element is a diner's complaint: the server, a wobbly table, the dish
(cold emails — the Monday special), and the bill (the whole weekend). The
joke only works for Monday: no other day serves cold emails and charges your
weekend. Nineteen words, no stage directions, no hashtags, no explanation.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (pure stdlib, deterministic, no network, no randomness) writes the
line to `/output/entry.txt`. Output: 100 characters, 19 words, 1 line, UTF-8,
within the 1–180 character gate and the 25-word cap.
